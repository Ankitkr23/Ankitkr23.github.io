I'm a final-year undergraduate at IIT Kanpur who loves turning noisy data into decisions. Most of my work sits where markets, machine learning and solid engineering meet.

This summer at Axxela Research & Analytics I researched a decade of Brent futures, built calendar-spread and fly strategies with PCA hedge ratios, and executed 300+ trades at 70%+ accuracy — earning a Pre-Placement Offer. I'm also a Research Consultant at WorldQuant, where I've submitted 50+ alphas.

Beyond finance I build ML systems — deepfake-detecting transformers, an agentic legal-RAG pipeline, a 0.6M-parameter audio turn detector — and backend tools like QueryLens. I placed 3rd globally on Kaggle, I'm a Codeforces Expert, and I've led 200+ students running IIT Kanpur's student elections.
